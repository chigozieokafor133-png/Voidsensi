DARK GAMING DESIGN

Explore the dark interface, neon-purple and cyan color palette, glowing accents and mobile-first layout used by Void’s Sensi’s.

THE VOID’S LOOK

The site uses a dark, high-contrast gaming style with neon-purple highlights and cyan accents. The goal is to keep the interface readable while giving it a futuristic gaming atmosphere.

Night background · #080810Neon purple · #8B5CF6Electric cyan · #22D3EEMint status · #65F2BD

GLASS-LIKE PANELS

Subtle borders, translucent surfaces, rounded corners and soft shadows help cards stand out without making the page too bright.

NEON HIGHLIGHTS

Purple marks key actions and cyan highlights links, numbers and interactive details. Use glow sparingly so important controls stay easy to spot.

READABILITY FIRST

Keep body text light against the dark background, maintain clear spacing, and make buttons large enough to tap on phones.

MOBILE-FRIENDLY

Cards stack on small screens, navigation collapses into a menu, and content stays usable without horizontal scrolling.
